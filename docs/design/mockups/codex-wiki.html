A river crossing and a market of about forty families. The Ferrymen of the Ford run the boats when the water is too high to wade. The Ford is the start of The Karrow Road.

The crossing

The river runs wide and shallow here for most of the year. In late spring the meltwater comes down from the pass and the ford can't be waded for weeks; the ferrymen charge double and nobody argues. East of the crossing the banks give way to the brackish flats of Saltmarsh Reach.

Before he kept the toll house, Old Hesk worked the boats here. He doesn't say why he stopped.

The market

Market day is the first of every week. Traders come down from the pass with wool and iron and go home with salt, fish and gossip.

Since Cloveswick

One survivor of Cloveswick, a boy who only says up, up, up, was taken in here. He points north, past the third waystone, towards Hollow Tor.
