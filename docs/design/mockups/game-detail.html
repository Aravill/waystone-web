The Sunken Crown

A drowned kingdom stirs beneath the Saltmarsh. Someone is diving for its crown, and the tide is turning red.

§ The tale

Three hundred years ago the kingdom of Ys slid beneath the waves in a single night. The fishermen of Saltmarsh still pull up coral-crusted coins in their nets, and nobody asks where they come from.

This spring the coins stopped. Instead the nets bring up bodies, every one of them wearing a circlet of black pearl. The town council wants it quiet. The temple of the Tide Mother wants it stopped. The smugglers in the sea caves under Gellan's Rock want to know who is cutting into their trade.

You are a crew of hired divers, disgraced sailors and curious scholars who took the council's coin. Expect investigation, ship-to-ship fights, a fair bit of swimming, and a dungeon that floods on a timer.

We play every other Thursday evening, roughly three hours. Levels 3 to 12, milestone levelling. New players welcome; I'll help with characters.
