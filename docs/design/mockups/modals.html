Every dialog in the app, redrawn so it reads at a glance. Same tokens, same faces and the same double frame, with fewer lines fighting and clearer zones. Use Look → Today to compare.

What makes them hard to read today

  1. The sheet melts into the page. It is the same --bg as the page under the backdrop, with no shadow, so in Candlelight its edge is only a thin line.
  2. Four parallel rules in the first 80px: the border, the outline 5px out and the 3px double header rule all compete with the content.
  3. An empty field is just a line. The ledger-line input reads like another divider (in the screenshot, the rubric line under "Folder name" looks like a rule, not a field).
  4. Explanations are small and muted. Confirm messages are 14px --muted monospace: the one thing you have to read is the faintest text.
  5. The buttons break the layout. The main button is .submit-btn at width: 100%, a bright slab in dark mode, so Cancel wraps underneath on the right. Every dialog orders its buttons differently.

The proposal

  1. Three zones: a --folio header band (eyebrow + 28px title, borderless × until hover), the --bg body, and a --folio footer for the actions. The body scrolls; header and footer stay put.
  2. Lift the sheet: darker scrim (rgba(43,36,28,.55) / rgba(0,0,0,.74)) and a soft shadow. The double frame stays, but the outer line drops to --line at 4px so it frames instead of competing. One ink rule under the header replaces the 3px double rule.
  3. Fields are ledger cells: the ink bottom line stays, the cell gets a --folio fill and 12px padding. Focus turns the frame rubric with a 2px rubric bottom line. Labels 15px/600, hints 14px under the field.
  4. Read text is ink, 16px, line-height 1.6. Muted stays for hints and meta only.
  5. One button order everywhere: right-aligned, Cancel then the main action (auto width, min 7rem); a destructive or secondary action (Delete game, Clear character, Manage templates) sits at the far left. On phones the sheet docks to the bottom and buttons stack full width, main action on top.
  6. Three widths: S 28rem (confirms, one or two fields), M 36rem (longer forms, lists), L 56rem (Codices New page, Templates, template editor).
  7. Codices dialogs wear the Codices pair (Inter + Barlow Condensed, from the font fix) and the Codices dark bar for the header, so they match the page they open from.

Two calls for you: (a) a destructive confirm ("Delete page") gets a rubric-filled button rather than the outlined danger button, because it is the one action in the dialog; (b) inputs in dialogs become filled ledger cells, which bends the guide's "a ledger line, not a box". Both show in every sheet below.
