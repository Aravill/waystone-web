§ The tale

Three hundred years ago the kingdom of Ys slid beneath the waves in a single night. The fishermen of Saltmarsh still pull up coral-crusted coins in their nets, and nobody asks where they come from.

This spring the coins stopped. Instead the nets bring up bodies, every one of them wearing a circlet of black pearl. The town council wants it quiet. The temple of the Tide Mother wants it stopped.

We play every other Thursday evening, roughly three hours. Levels 3 to 12, milestone levelling. New players welcome; I'll help with characters.
