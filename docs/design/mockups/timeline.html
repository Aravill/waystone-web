      Decisions in this design

      • A page type, not a new screen. A timeline is a codex page with a type (the unused kind column becomes article / timeline, later map). It keeps everything a page has: title, folder, tags, aliases, history, links, the intro text. Only the part under the intro changes with the type.
      • An event is a title, a date and maybe a page. Linked by page id, so a renamed page renames the event. Linked events show the page's first lines and tags; unlinked ones are just a title. If the linked page is deleted, the event keeps its last title and loses the link.
      • Dates are the world's, not ours. Year (required, whole number), month and day optional, sorted newest first. Same year without a month sorts at the bottom of that year ("sometime in 1203").
      • Gaps show time passing. More than 30 years between two years dashes the spine and says "338 years earlier". Shorter gaps stay solid.
      • State is never colour alone. Filled square: has a page. Hollow square: no page. Double-ruled square: a year.
      • Editing events saves at once (like images and tags), each change as a new revision of the timeline page so History can show and restore it.
      • Open: month names (here a world calendar with 12 named months and an "AR" era, set once per timeline under More › Calendar), or plain numbers ("3/1203")? Years before 0?
      • Open: the "Create a page for it" box in Add event (makes an Event-template page and links it). Keep or drop?
